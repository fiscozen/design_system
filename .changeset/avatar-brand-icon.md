'@fiscozen/avatar': minor

`FzAvatar` takes `iconName` and `iconVariant` to draw a Font Awesome icon in place of the initials, for an avatar that stands for a brand or a service, such as the Fiscozen mark (`iconName="fiscozen" iconVariant="fak"`). The icon is sized to the environment's circle. On its own it is exposed as an image named after `firstName` and `lastName`; next to a `title` it is hidden from assistive technology, since the title already names it.

`@fiscozen/icons` is now a peer dependency of `@fiscozen/avatar`.
